METHODOLOGY / TRANSPARENCY

How AtlasIQ turns signals into decisions.

This page describes the static browser release. It is a decision-support prototype, not an official university advising system, clinical tool, or production model-validation platform.

Campus scoring

Combines user-selected course characteristics and time constraints into transparent balance factors. Scores are heuristic decision aids. The interface should expose factor contributions rather than treat the result as ground truth.

Ripple Engine

Uses explicit bounded assumptions to estimate directional tradeoffs between workload, free time, career readiness, and resilience. Ranges communicate uncertainty; results are counterfactual estimates, not causal guarantees.

Career + Opportunity Radar

Compares normalized skill and evidence signals against role or posting requirements. Keyword coverage is useful for prioritization but does not predict hiring outcomes.

Studio analytics

Runs browser-native data profiling, correlation discovery, simple model comparison, deterministic holdout evaluation, feature influence summaries, and what-if analysis. Lightweight models are designed for explainability and demonstration, not state-of-the-art benchmark performance.

Atlas Nexus

Aggregates already-visible workspace signals into a priority score and next-best-action recommendation. Nexus does not invent a new hidden score; it is a routing layer over the platform’s existing evidence.

Privacy model

Profile data and working state remain in the browser in the static release. Public datasets are retrieved separately and only enter the private workspace when the visitor explicitly loads them into Studio.
